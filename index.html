Our Mission & Heart

What is Outspire?

Our startup brings strangers together in real-world spaces and turns unfamiliar moments into lively conversations, shared laughter, and lasting friendships.

In a world where social circles often stay small and online interactions replace real conversations, we create opportunities for people to meet face to face wherever they want to gather — whether that's at a café, restaurant, playground, park, going for a walk, or any other place or activity members choose. Meetups are never limited to just one kind of venue; they happen wherever people feel like connecting.

We thoughtfully bring together people in welcoming, comfortable gatherings where conversations flow naturally and without pressure. The focus is never on speed, performance, or forced networking, but on genuine human interaction — talking, listening, sharing stories, and slowly getting to know one another.

Whether you are hosting or joining a meetup or event, Outspire is about turning unfamiliar faces into familiar ones and helping real friendships grow from simple, authentic real-world experiences.

Zero Pressure Guarantee

Your comfort and enjoyment are our first priority

Meeting new people requires psychological safety. Here is how we ensure every single gathering feels safe, respectful, and completely comfortable.

📍

100% Safe, Open Public Places

Meetups happen wherever people love to gather—cafés, restaurants, playgrounds, parks, scenic walks, or community spaces. Every gathering takes place in open, trusted public spaces, ensuring complete safety, comfort, and peace of mind.

🛡️

Post-Meetup Safe Departure

When a meetup ends, every attendee confirms in their meetup profile that they have safely moved out and departed the venue. Hosts and circle members verify everyone is safe before the meetup concludes.

⏱️

3-Hour Fair Cancellation

Cancel free with a single tap up to 3 hours before the gathering starts. To protect hosts and ensure tables aren't left waiting, no cancellations are permitted within 3 hours.

🤝

Zero Tolerance Code of Conduct

Harassment, unsolicited sales pitches, or pushy behavior result in immediate account removal. Every member agrees to our mutual respect pledge before booking.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Got a question before joining your first table? Here are answers to the most common questions from our community.

What if I feel awkward or shy meeting strangers?
Feeling nervous is completely natural! That's why our tables are sized comfortably from 2 to 10 people, and our verified hosts gently break the ice. You don't have to entertain the table or speak non-stop; listening and enjoying the atmosphere is equally valued.
How does paying for food and beverages work?
When you meet at the restaurant or café, each attendee simply covers their own personal food or beverage order directly with the venue. We don't pre-book dining packages or enforce set menus.
How does the 3-hour cancellation policy work?
You can cancel your reservation with a single click in your dashboard up to 3 hours before the gathering starts. Within 3 hours of the meetup, cancellations lock so that hosts and fellow members are not left with empty tables.
How does the Safe Departure Check-In work?
Your safety matters before, during, and after the meetup. When your gathering concludes, every attendee simply taps "Confirm Safe Departure" in their meetup profile. This notifies the host and fellow circle members that you've safely left the venue, giving everyone total peace of mind.
What happens after I reserve a seat?
Once your seat is reserved, you unlock the private Table Group Chat! You can view fellow attendees, say a quick hello, and receive exact venue details and directions.
Who selects the meetup locations and venues?
Hosts and members can choose any verified public spot they love—whether it's a neighborhood café, restaurant, playground, park, scenic walking route, or community space. We ensure every spot is safe, easy to find, and welcoming for conversation.
